# Business decision note

Copy this file for each pull request and rename the copy to `YYYY-MM-DD-short-topic.md`.

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Related PR:** link or number
- **Context:** What business question or need prompted this change?
- **Decision:** What was decided? If no business decision was needed, write “No business decisions were required.”
- **Rationale:** Why was this option chosen?
- **Impact:** What users, business outcomes, scope, or trade-offs does it affect?
